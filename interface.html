🎓 Admission at DIMS College

✅ Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board with minimum required marks depending on the course.

📅 Important Dates

  • Application Start: 15th May 2025
  • Last Date to Apply: 30th July 2025
  • Entrance Test (if any): 5th August 2025
  • Admission Confirmation: 15th August 2025

📄 Documents Required

  • 10th & 12th Marksheet (Photocopy)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Passport Size Photos (4)

📝 Admission Procedure

1. Visit the official website or campus.
2. Fill out the admission form online/offline.
3. Upload/submit the required documents.
4. Pay the registration fee.
5. Wait for confirmation from the admission office.

DIMS College was established in 2000 with a vision to make higher education accessible to all in the region. Since then, it has grown to become a reputed institution.

🏛️ Our History

DIMS College was founded with a mission to create a meaningful educational legacy. Here's a glimpse of our journey:

🎓 2005 – Foundation

Established with a vision to deliver quality education in Meerut. Started with just 2 departments and a few classrooms.

🏗️ 2010 – Expansion

New academic blocks, advanced computer labs, and a digital library were added to meet growing demands.

🌐 2016 – Digital Leap

Introduced smart classrooms, online learning tools, and partnered with tech-driven institutions.

🏆 2020 – Recognition

Awarded for excellence in education by regional bodies and ranked among the top colleges in Uttar Pradesh.

🚀 2025 – Future Ready

Now equipped with AI labs, research centers, and a dynamic curriculum ready for global challenges.
